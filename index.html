Privacy Policy — Learn DS and Algorithms

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Learn DS and Algorithms ("the App") is an educational mobile application designed to help users learn data structures and algorithms through lessons, examples, visualizations, and practice questions. This policy explains how the current version of the App handles information.

2. Information We Handle

The current version stores certain learning progress information locally on your device, such as completed lesson identifiers and accumulated experience points (XP). This information is used to save your progress. It is not transmitted to our servers by the current version.

The App does not require an account, name, email address, or password to use its current learning features.

3. Data Collection and Sharing

The current version does not include advertising or analytics services. We do not operate a server that receives your learning progress, and we do not sell or share that progress with third parties.

Your device or operating system may independently process technical information according to its own policies and settings.

4. Data Storage and Deletion

Learning progress is stored locally on your device. It remains there until it is deleted or the App's local data is cleared. You can generally remove it by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the App. Uninstalling may permanently remove locally stored progress.

We do not maintain a separate server-side copy of this learning progress in the current version.

5. Data Security

The App relies on the local storage and security mechanisms provided by your device and operating system. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not require users to submit personal information to access its current learning features. For questions or concerns about privacy, please contact us using the details below.

7. Third-Party Services

The current version does not integrate advertising or analytics SDKs. If third-party services are introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated as necessary to explain relevant data practices.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App's features or data practices change. Updates will be published at this URL, with a revised last-updated date.
